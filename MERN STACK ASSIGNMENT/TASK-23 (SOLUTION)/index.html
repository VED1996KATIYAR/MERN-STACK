MORE THAN A BUILDING FULL OF BOOKS - A HOME OF CURIOUS MINDS
Greenwood Public Library has been at the heart of this community since 1983.We believe everyone deserves free access to knowledge,regardless of age income,or background .Our six sections cover everything from fiction to digital media.
Beyond books,we host weekly events- reading challenges for childer,book clubs ,digital literacy, workshops, and author meets.Our study rooms,free wi-fi and welcoming staff make Green Wood a place come back to year after year.

Reddit uses a variant of markdown. So: Two linebreaks for new paragraph two spaces at end of line for newline (which is not the same as new paragraph) headlines are prefixed with a hash, number of hashes coresponds to "level" of headline (i.e. one hash is top level, two is below that, and so on) underscores or stars for bold and italics (one is italics, two is bold) and so on. If you want to escape a character you can prefix it with a
